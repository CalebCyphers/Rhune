# Move summaries — draft
Source of truth: rhune-ttrpg/RULES/moves/*.md + HOW-TO-PLAY.md (stat-less rules).
Format: the **"when" trigger only** — one line. Mechanics live on the site (link).

## Batch 1 — Basic + Special (10)

1. **Clash** — When you fight in melee or close quarters
2. **Defend** — When you take up a defensive stance or jump in to protect others
3. **Defy Danger** — When danger looms and you do something chancy (no other move applies)
4. **Know Things** — When you consult your accumulated knowledge
5. **Let Fly** — When you take a shot with a ranged weapon
6. **Persuade (vs. NPCs)** — When you press or entice an NPC who has reason to resist
7. **Seek Insight** — When you study a situation or person for insight
8. **Advantage / Disadvantage** — When you roll with advantage or disadvantage
9. **Burn Brightly** — After any of your rolls, when you have enough XP to Level Up
10. **Death's Door** — When you're dying (0 HP from a killing blow)
